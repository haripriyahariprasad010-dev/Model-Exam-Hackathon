Discover Amazing Marine Life

Marine life includes all the plants and animals that live in oceans and seas. Each creature plays an important role in maintaining the underwater ecosystem.

Sea Turtles

Sea turtles are marine reptiles that travel long distances through the ocean. They play an important role in marine ecosystems.

Dolphins

Dolphins are intelligent marine mammals known for their communication and playful behaviour.

Colourful Fish

Tropical fish live in different ocean habitats, especially in colourful coral reef environments.

Whales

Whales are among the largest animals on Earth. They play an important role in maintaining healthy marine ecosystems.
