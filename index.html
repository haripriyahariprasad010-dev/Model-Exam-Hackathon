Welcome to the Underwater World!

Oceans cover most of our planet and are home to thousands of amazing living creatures. From tiny colourful fish to enormous whales, the underwater world is full of beauty and mystery.

Why Are Oceans Important?

Oceans help regulate the Earth's climate, provide food and support a wide variety of marine life. They are also important for transportation and the balance of nature.

  • Oceans provide a home for marine animals.
  • They help maintain the Earth's temperature.
  • They provide food and natural resources.
  • They support biodiversity and ecosystems.
