Ocean Wonders – Marine Life & Ocean Exploration

Ocean Wonders is a simple website created to explore the beauty of oceans and learn about marine life. It provides information about different sea animals, ocean exploration and ways to protect our oceans.

Pages Included

Home: Introduction to the ocean world.

Marine Life: Learn about sea turtles, dolphins, fish and whales.

Ocean Guide: Explore ocean protection tips, images, audio and a PDF guide.
