Ocean Exploration Tips

  1. Always follow safety instructions.
  2. Never touch or disturb marine animals.
  3. Avoid throwing waste into the ocean.
  4. Respect coral reefs and underwater habitats.

Ways to Protect Our Oceans

  • Reduce the use of plastic.
  • Keep beaches clean.
  • Protect marine animals.
  • Spread awareness about ocean conservation.
